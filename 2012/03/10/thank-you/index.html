Thank you!

March 10, 2012

As many of you may know, I am getting married.

 

Well, tomorrow is the big day!

I want to thank everyone who pitched in and supported me (read: us). Come April, you should expect to see news updates rolling in. But for now, I’m going to enjoy this as much as I can.

For the past couple of months, donations have been my only source of income. It’s not easy trying to plan a wedding and prepare for a new life without a steady income. I’m happy to saw we have been able to budget a wedding, apartment furnishings, and honeymoon without having to be swimming in debt. It’s amazing how helpful you guys have been!

 

Thank you, once again!!!
